This document explains how a lawfully acquired physical mannaNAUTA board may
be used. It does not authorize reproduction or manufacture of the board or its
distinctive visual shape.

1. use the board in personal, educational, academic, scientific, artistic,
   prototyping, and research projects;
2. use the board as a component of commercial projects, installations,
   services, and products;
3. develop, run, publish, and distribute software or firmware intended for the
   board, subject to the licenses of the software and third-party dependencies;
4. publish photographs, videos, demonstrations, tutorials, reviews, and
   project documentation showing the lawfully acquired board; and
5. use truthful descriptions such as "built with mannaNAUTA" or "compatible
   with mannaNAUTA".

## Unauthorized uses

This permission does not authorize anyone to:

1. manufacture, commission the manufacture of, or sell replicas of the

2. represent a modified, derived, or third-party board as an original
   mannaNAUTA product;
3. use non-public manufacturing or design files without written permission;
4. claim certification, warranty, support, partnership, or endorsement that
   has not been expressly granted in writing.
